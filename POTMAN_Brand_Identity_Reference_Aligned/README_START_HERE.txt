LOGO FIDELITY
Wordmark contours were reconstructed from the primary-logo area of your uploaded image, preserving the open P counter, half-circle O and geometric letterforms.
As this source is a compressed raster-style image, the vector outline is a close reference-aligned reconstruction, not a claim to the unknown original Illustrator curves. Inspect before final print tooling.
The small circled-R in the reference is deliberately omitted from delivered production logos until registered-trademark status is verified.
